# Meio Auto Run Turn

Steer with your strafe keys while autorunning: A and D turn you instead of sidestepping.

For WoW Forever.

![Mockup, not a game screenshot: seen from above, autorunning with A held. Without Meio Auto
Run Turn you drift diagonally forward and left, off course; with it you curve left and keep
running](docs/turning.png)

## What it does

While you autorun, the keys you strafe with (A and D, or whatever you bound to Strafe Left
and Strafe Right) and the Left and Right arrows turn your character, so you can steer a long
run without the mouse. Stop autorunning and they strafe again.

- Starts with your Autorun key (Num Lock by default); ends when you press forward or back,
  autorun again, or stop moving
- Strafes as normal in combat: keys switch back when a fight starts
- Uses your own key bindings, nothing is rebound for good

No settings, no slash commands.

## Install

Use your addon manager (CurseForge, Wago), or unzip a GitHub release into
`World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns` and restart WoW fully.
